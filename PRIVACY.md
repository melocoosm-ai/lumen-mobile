# Lumen Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Lumen is a web browser for iOS and Android. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: **Lumen does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any
personal data.**

## What stays on your device

Lumen saves the following **only on your device**, so the app works the way you left it:

- The web addresses of your open tabs
- Bookmarks you create

This data never leaves your device through Lumen. You can delete it at any time by closing tabs,
removing bookmarks (long-press a bookmark), or uninstalling the app.

## What Lumen does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs
- No crash reporting to us
- No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos
- No third-party cookies (they are blocked)

## Websites you visit

When you open a website, your device connects directly to that website, like any browser. That
website (and services it uses) may collect information under its own privacy policy. Lumen has no
control over third-party sites.

Searches typed into the address bar are sent to DuckDuckGo
(<https://duckduckgo.com/privacy>), which does not track searches.

## Children

Lumen does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
